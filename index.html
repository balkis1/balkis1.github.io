About me

I'm an AI engineer who works where machine learning meets the life sciences. Over the last two years I've taken models from research notebooks into production systems, in genomics, bioinformatics and medicinal chemistry.

Most recently I designed and built a medicinal chemistry copilot: several specialist open-source LLMs answer in parallel, a judge model picks the strongest answer, and RDKit makes sure every molecule it proposes is chemically valid.

Before that I spent two and a half years at Vitafluence.ai fine-tuning protein language models, building mRNA design APIs and working with University College London on a peptide delivery project that became a conference poster.

What I enjoy most: turning a promising model into something people can rely on, with the evaluation to prove it.
